SELLER INTAKE CHECKLIST — BEFORE RUNNING ADS

Get these confirmed before spending money on ads:

- VIN or partial VIN
- Current mileage
- Exterior color
- Interior color
- Wheel setup
- Title status or payoff status
- Any liens
- Accident/paintwork disclosure
- Tire condition
- Software screen showing mileage/options
- FSD/subscription status
- Premium Connectivity status
- Photo folder link
- Walkaround video link
- Written agreement on public asking price
- Written agreement on your compensation/markup
- Written agreement that leads come to you first
- Written agreement on ad budget and who pays it
- Written exclusive marketing window, even if short
